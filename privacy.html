Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18

HandsomeSpotted Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool operated by a single individual to upload videos to a YouTube channel that the same individual owns.

What the app accesses

The app requests three Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — used solely to upload videos with their titles, descriptions, tags and privacy/schedule settings to the owner's own channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — used solely to confirm which of the owner's channels a video is being uploaded to and to list the owner's recent uploads.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube — used solely to set or clear the scheduled publish time of the owner's own private videos.

What the app collects

The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer any personal data or Google user data to anyone. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only by the owner.

Third parties

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
